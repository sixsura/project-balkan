Project Balkan - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Project Balkan Discord bot handles information when it is used on a Discord server.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features being used, the Bot may process limited Discord information necessary to operate its commands.

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server (guild) ID
  • Discord channel ID
  • Discord interaction and command information

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account tokens, payment information, or private messages for unrelated purposes.

2. How Information Is Used

Information available to the Bot is used only as necessary to respond to commands, provide Bot functionality, identify the Discord server where a command is being used, maintain the Bot, and protect it against abuse.

3. Data Storage

The Bot is designed to minimize data collection. Information is not intentionally sold or provided to third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Discord and Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with third-party services required for its functionality. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the Bot, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information used by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the Project Balkan administration through the official Project Balkan Discord server.
